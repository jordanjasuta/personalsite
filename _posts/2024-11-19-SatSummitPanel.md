[Panel summary](https://satsummit.io/2024-lisbon/agenda/#2024-lisbon-18-1400-future-climate-weather): AI is causing a paradigm shift in Climate and Weather modeling, with a move from numerical to data-driven approaches. This panel will explore the integration of AI into these scientific domains. How do we build trust and drive adoption of AI models? What will be the role of numerical approaches in the future? Are there lessons that apply to other scientific domains?

Role: [Moderator](https://satsummit.io/2024-lisbon/speakers/jordan-fischer/)

Panelists:
[Andreas Schlueter, PhD](https://satsummit.io/2024-lisbon/speakers/andreas-schlueter/)
[Clemens Cremer, PhD](https://satsummit.io/2024-lisbon/speakers/clemens-cremer/)
[Emanuel Dutra, PhD](https://satsummit.io/2024-lisbon/speakers/emanuel-dutra/)
[Rahul Ramachandran, PhD](https://satsummit.io/2024-lisbon/speakers/rahul-ramachandran/)

----------

As part of the Mexican public agency CONEVAL's Evaluation Week gLOCAL 2024, participants in this panel (roughly translated as "AI for the generation of public policy evidence - responsible innovation and ethical challenges"), discussed the responsible use of AI for public policy evaluation.

The panel recording can be viewed on [youtube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=C7kxp1dJWdU&ab_channel=CONEVALVIDEO) or [facebook live](https://www.facebook.com/Coneval/videos/1245388350102775).
